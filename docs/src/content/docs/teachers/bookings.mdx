When a family books one of your time slots, the conference appears on your dashboard and on your time slot calendar. This page shows where to find each booking, what the family told you, and how to cancel a conference.

## Who this is for

Teachers and staff who have conference time slots.

## Before you start

- You need time slots that families can book. See [Creating your time slots](/teachers/creating-slots/).

## See your upcoming conferences

The **My conferences** card on the dashboard lists every upcoming booked conference. Under the heading it tells you how many open time slots remain, for example "4 open time slots remaining."

<Screenshot name="dashboard-teacher" alt="The My conferences card listing each booked conference with its time, student, contact and notes" />

| Column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **When** | The date and time of the conference. |
| **Student** | The student the conference is about. |
| **Contact** | The parent or guardian who booked it. |
| **Notes** | What the family wrote when booking. |

If nothing is booked yet you see **No upcoming conferences.**

## See booking details on the calendar

<Steps>
1. Open **Time Slots** in the sidebar. Booked slots show the student's name.
   <Screenshot name="time-slots-teacher" alt="The teacher's time slot calendar with booked slots labeled by student name" />
2. To see full details, go to **Manage my time slots** and click a booked slot. The **Edit time slot** window opens with a **Reservation** section.
3. The **Reservation** section shows the student, the contact's name and email, when it was booked (**Booked** followed by the date), the family's **Notes**, whether they asked to meet online (**Online requested**), and the **Translator** language if they requested one.
4. Open **History** at the bottom to see every change to the slot.
</Steps>

If a translator was requested, you can add **Translator notes**. These are visible to staff only, for example the assigned translator.

## Cancel a conference

You can cancel a booking from the dashboard or from the calendar. The family and you both get a cancellation email (if notifications are on), and the slot becomes open again.

<Steps>
1. On the dashboard, find the conference in **My conferences** and select **Cancel**.
2. Confirm with **Cancel conference**.
</Steps>

From the calendar, open the booked slot and select **Cancel reservation**, then confirm.

<Aside type="note">
Booked slots cannot be moved or deleted. Cancel the reservation first if you need to remove the slot. If you only change details such as the location or meeting link, the family keeps the booking and is emailed the new details.
</Aside>
