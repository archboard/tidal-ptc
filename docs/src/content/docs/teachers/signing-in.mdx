Teachers sign in to Tidal PTC with their PowerSchool account or, if your district allows it, with an email and password. After you sign in you land on the dashboard, which lists your upcoming conferences.

## Who this is for

Teachers and other staff who meet with families. Your account comes from PowerSchool, so you do not need to register.

## Before you start

- Your district must have set up Tidal PTC and synced staff from PowerSchool.
- The sign-in options you see depend on your district's settings: **Sign in with PowerSchool**, an email and password form, or both.
- If your district uses the hosted version, you may first be asked for a **District code**. Ask your school office for it.

## Sign in with PowerSchool

<Steps>
1. Open your district's Tidal PTC address.
   <Screenshot name="login" alt="The Tidal PTC sign-in page with the Sign in with PowerSchool button above the email and password form" />
2. Optionally check **Keep me logged in**.
3. Select **Sign in with PowerSchool** and sign in with your PowerSchool staff account.
4. You return to the Tidal PTC dashboard.
</Steps>

## Sign in with email and password

<Steps>
1. Under **Log in as**, choose **Staff**.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Optionally check **Keep me logged in**, then select **Log in**.
</Steps>

If you forgot your password, select **Forgot your password?**, choose **Staff** under **Account type**, enter your email and select **Email Password Reset Link**.

<Aside type="note">
Your name and email sync from PowerSchool. Changes you make to them in Tidal PTC may be overwritten on the next sync or the next time you sign in.
</Aside>

## Choose your school

If your district has more than one school, Tidal PTC asks you to **Choose your current school** the first time you sign in. If you have access to more than one school, a school menu at the top of the sidebar lets you switch later. Time slots and conferences always belong to the school you have selected.

## Your dashboard

The dashboard shows whether booking is open for families (for example **Booking is open.** or **Booking is closed.**) and a **My conferences** card with your upcoming booked conferences and how many open time slots you have left.
