You can use Tidal PTC in English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish or Arabic. Conference times show in your own timezone, so it is worth checking that the timezone is right before you book.

## Who this is for

Anyone using Tidal PTC, especially families who prefer a language other than English or who live in a different timezone from the school.

## Change the language

The language menu is near the top of the sidebar, under the logo. On a phone, open the menu first.

<Steps>
1. Open the language menu. Each language is listed in its own writing: English, 简体中文, 日本語, 한국어, Español, العربية.
2. Choose your language. The page reloads in that language.
</Steps>

Your choice is saved to your account, so emails from Tidal PTC also arrive in that language. When you choose Arabic, pages read from right to left.

You can also change it in **Personal settings** under **Language**.

## Check your timezone

If your device's timezone is different from the one saved in your account, Tidal PTC asks you about it:

<Steps>
1. A window called **Update your timezone** asks if you want to change to your device's timezone. Select **Ok** to accept it.
2. To pick a different one, select **Choose something else**, choose your **Timezone**, then select **Ok**.
</Steps>

A yellow bar at the bottom of the page may also ask "Are you in the ... timezone?" Select **No, change it** to fix it.

## Change settings in Personal settings

<Steps>
1. Open **Personal settings** from the sidebar.
   <Screenshot name="settings-personal" alt="Personal settings for a parent with Timezone, Language and time format options" />
2. Under **Personal details**, set your **Timezone** and **Language**.
3. Check **Format time using 24 hours** if you prefer times like 13:00 instead of 1:00pm.
4. Check **Use dark theme** if you prefer a dark screen. This is saved in this browser only.
5. Select **Save**.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Your name and email come from the school's records. If you change them here, they may change back the next time the school's information updates. Ask the school to update them instead.
</Aside>
