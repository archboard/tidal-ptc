You book a conference by choosing a teacher on your dashboard and picking one of their open times. You can move it to another time or cancel it later. You get an email each time a conference is booked, moved or cancelled.

## Who this is for

Parents and guardians booking conferences for their children.

## Before you start

- [Sign in](/guardians/signing-in/) and find your child on the dashboard. See [Choosing a student](/guardians/choosing-a-student/).
- Booking must be open. The top of your dashboard tells you, for example **Booking is closed** with the date it opens, or the date booking closes.
- Check that times show in your own timezone. See [Language and timezone](/guardians/language-and-timezone/).

## Book a conference

<Steps>
1. On the dashboard, find your child and the teacher, then select **Book**.
   <Screenshot name="dashboard-guardian" alt="The family dashboard with a Book link next to a teacher in the child's list" />
2. A window opens with your child's and the teacher's names. It says **Choose a time that works for you.**
3. Pick a time. **List** shows the open times grouped by day. **Calendar** shows them on a calendar, where taken times are gray and marked **Booked**, and times that clash with another of your conferences are marked **Conflict**.
4. Under the time you chose, add any **Notes** for the teacher (for example, anything you would like to discuss).
5. If you see them, you can also check **I would like to meet online** (see [Online meetings](/guardians/online-meetings/)) or choose a language under **Translator** (see [Requesting a translator](/guardians/translators/)).
6. Select **Book conference**. You see "Conference booked successfully." and the conference appears under **Your conferences** at the top of the dashboard.
</Steps>

If the window says **There are no available times right now.**, all of the teacher's times are taken or too soon to book.

## See your conferences

The **Your conferences** list at the top of the dashboard shows **When**, **Student**, **Teacher** and **Where** for every upcoming conference. **Where** shows the room, **Online**, or a **Join video call** link.

## Move a conference to a different time

<Steps>
1. On the dashboard, find the booked teacher in your child's list and select **Edit**.
2. The window says when the conference is currently booked. Your current time is marked **Current**.
3. Pick a new time, check your notes, then select **Move conference**.
</Steps>

You can only move a conference to another time with the same teacher, and only while booking is open.

## Cancel a conference

<Steps>
1. On the dashboard, find the booked teacher in your child's list and select **Cancel**.
2. Confirm with **Cancel conference**. You see "Conference cancelled."
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
Close to the conference time, you can no longer cancel online. If you see "This reservation can no longer be cancelled online. Please contact the school.", call or email the school instead.
</Aside>

## Booking rules

These rules keep the schedule fair. If one applies, the window shows a message explaining why.

- **One conference per teacher.** Each child can have one upcoming conference with each teacher. To change it, move it instead of booking again.
- **No double booking.** You cannot pick a time that overlaps another conference for the same child, or another conference you booked for any of your children.
- **Not too close to the start.** The school sets a number of hours before each conference when it can no longer be booked or cancelled online. Those times do not appear.
- **First come, first served.** If someone else books a time just before you, you see "This time slot has already been reserved." Pick another time.
- **Booking window.** You can only book, and move, conferences between the dates the school opens and closes booking.
