Some schools let you meet teachers by video call instead of in person. You can ask for an online meeting when you book, and the video call link shows on your dashboard and in your emails.

## Who this is for

Parents and guardians who would like to meet a teacher online.

## Before you start

- Your school must allow online meetings. If it does not, you will not see the online option.
- Each teacher decides which of their times can be online. Some times are online only.

## Ask to meet online

<Steps>
1. Start [booking a conference](/guardians/booking/) and pick a time.
2. Check **I would like to meet online**. If you do not see it, that time is in person only.
3. Select **Book conference**.
</Steps>

If the teacher made the time online only, your conference is online even if you did not check the box.

## Join the video call

<Steps>
1. Go to **Dashboard** and find the conference under **Your conferences**.
   <Screenshot name="dashboard-guardian" alt="The Your conferences list with a Join video call link in the Where column" />
2. In the **Where** column, select **Join video call** at the time of your conference. The call opens in a new tab.
</Steps>

If **Where** says **Online** without a link, the teacher has not added a link yet. When they do, you get an email with the new details (if that email is turned on, see [Reminders and emails](/guardians/reminders/)). Your reminder email also includes the link when there is one.

<Aside type="tip">
Test your camera and microphone a few minutes before the conference starts.
</Aside>
