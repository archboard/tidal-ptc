You sign in to Tidal PTC with the same account you use for your school's PowerSchool parent portal, or with an email and password if your school offers that. Once you are in, you see your children and their teachers on one page.

## Who this is for

Parents, guardians and other family contacts who want to book a conference with a teacher.

## Before you start

- Your school gives you the web address for Tidal PTC.
- Your account comes from the school's records, so you do not need to sign up. Use the email address the school has for you.
- Some schools ask for a four-character **District code** first. If you see that box, type the code the school gave you and select **Continue**.

## Sign in with PowerSchool

<Steps>
1. Open the Tidal PTC address your school gave you.
   <Screenshot name="login" alt="The sign-in page with a Sign in with PowerSchool button and an email and password form" />
2. If you want to stay signed in on this device, check **Keep me logged in**.
3. Select **Sign in with PowerSchool** and sign in with your parent portal account.
4. You come back to Tidal PTC, signed in.
</Steps>

## Sign in with email and password

<Steps>
1. Under **Log in as**, choose **Contact/Guardian**.
2. Type your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Log in**.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Make sure **Contact/Guardian** is selected. The page starts with **Staff** chosen, and your password will not work if the wrong one is picked.
</Aside>

## Forgot your password

<Steps>
1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot your password?**
2. Under **Account type**, choose **Contact/Guardian**.
3. Type your **Email** and select **Email Password Reset Link**.
4. Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.
</Steps>

You can change your password later in **Personal settings**, under **Security settings**.

## If you see "No students found"

This means your account is not linked to any students yet. Contact your child's school and ask them to check your contact information. Select **Log out** to leave the page.
