You sign in to Tidal PTC with the same account you use for your school's PowerSchool parent portal. You can start from the parent portal itself or from the Tidal PTC address your school gave you. Once you are in, you see your children and their teachers on one page.

## Who this is for

Parents, guardians and other family contacts who want to book a conference with a teacher.

## Before you start

- You need a PowerSchool parent portal account. Your account comes from the school's records, so you do not need to sign up.
- Some schools ask for a four-character **District code** first. If you see that box, type the code the school gave you and select **Continue**.

## Sign in from the PowerSchool parent portal

<Steps>
1. Sign in to the PowerSchool parent portal and select the **Applications** icon in the blue bar at the top of the page.
   <Screenshot name="powerschool-apps-button" alt="The PowerSchool parent portal header with the Applications icon circled in the blue bar" />
2. In the **Applications** panel, select **Tidal PTC**.
   <Screenshot name="powerschool-apps-list" alt="The PowerSchool Applications panel listing Tidal PTC, described as Create and reserve parent-teacher conferences" />
3. Tidal PTC opens, already signed in.
</Steps>

## Sign in from the Tidal PTC address

<Steps>
1. Open the Tidal PTC address your school gave you.
   <Screenshot name="login" alt="The Tidal PTC sign-in page with a Sign in with PowerSchool button" />
2. Select **Sign in with PowerSchool** and sign in with your parent portal account.
3. You come back to Tidal PTC, signed in.
</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
Some schools also allow an email and password. If yours does, the sign-in page shows a form below **Or continue with**: choose **Contact/Guardian** under **Log in as**, then enter your email and password. **Forgot your password?** sends a reset link.
</Aside>

## If you see "No students found"

This means your account is not linked to any students yet. Contact your child's school and ask them to check your contact information. Select **Log out** to leave the page.
