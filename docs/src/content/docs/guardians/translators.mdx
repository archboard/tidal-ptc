If you would like help with language during a conference, you can ask for a translator when you book. The school then arranges a translator for the language you chose.

## Who this is for

Parents and guardians who would like a translator at their conference.

## Before you start

- Your school must offer translators. If it does not, you will not see the **Translator** choice when booking.
- The school decides which languages it offers. Only those languages appear in the list.
- Some times may not allow translator requests. The **Translator** choice only appears for times that do.

## Ask for a translator

<Steps>
1. Start [booking a conference](/guardians/booking/) and pick a time.
2. Under **Translator** ("Request a translator for this conference."), choose your language. Leave it on **No translator needed** if you do not need one.
3. Finish your booking with **Book conference**.
</Steps>

To change or remove your request, select **Edit** on the dashboard, pick a new time, set the **Translator** choice and select **Move conference**. To keep the same time but change the request, contact the school.

## If no translator is available

Schools have a limited number of translators. You may see one of these messages:

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No more translator requests are available for this language." | The school has reached its limit for that language. Contact the school. |
| "No translator is available for this language at that time." | Every translator for that language is busy at that time. Pick a different time. |

## Confirmation

Your booking email says "Translator requested" with your language. If the school has already assigned a translator when a later email about the conference is sent (for example, when it is moved), that email shows the translator's name.
