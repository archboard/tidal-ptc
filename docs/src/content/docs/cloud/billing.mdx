Cloud and Private dedicated hosting are bought through the Archboard billing portal. Your purchase sets your district's subscription dates and the number of schools it is licensed for. This page covers buying, renewing, adding schools, and what happens if a subscription lapses.

## Who this is for

District administrators and purchasers. Self-hosted installs are free and need no purchase.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price per year (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud | $400 + $150 per school, up to $4,000 for a district. A single school pays $450. |
| Private dedicated | $880 + $150 per school, up to $4,480 for a district. A single school pays $930. |

Cloud servers are shared, so the cost of running them for each district goes down as more districts join. As that happens, Archboard will lower Cloud prices.

Private dedicated is the Cloud price plus $480 a year for your district's own server. That covers what the server costs Archboard plus payment processing fees. The rest of the price pays for the same managed service as Cloud: updates, backups, monitoring and support.

See [Choosing hosting](/getting-started/choosing-hosting/) for what each tier includes.

## Buy hosting

<Steps>
1. Go to the [billing portal](https://billing.archboard.io/get-started).
2. Buy Cloud or Private dedicated hosting for the number of schools you need.
3. After your purchase, Archboard creates your district with its own address, a [district code](/cloud/district-lookup/), your school limit and your subscription dates.
4. Use the setup and plugin links you receive to connect PowerSchool and create your first admin. See [Setup links](/cloud/setup-links/).
</Steps>

## Renew

Renew through the [billing portal](https://billing.archboard.io/get-started) before your subscription expires. Renewing updates your district's expiry date and school limit; your data, address and settings stay the same.

If you renew for fewer schools than you have active, staff are locked out until you deactivate the extras. See [School limits](/cloud/school-limits/#when-the-district-is-over-its-limit).

## Add schools

To use more schools than your license covers, buy more school licenses through the billing portal. When your district is over its limit, district admins may also see a **Request more licenses** link on the **At full capacity** page. Once the new limit is in place, activate the schools in [Tenant settings](/district-admin/tenant-settings/).

## If an invoice isn't paid

Invoices are due 30 days after they're sent, and your district keeps using Tidal PTC while you pay. If an invoice for your subscription is still unpaid on its due date, a 10-day grace period starts. Your district keeps working normally during the grace period.

Archboard emails your billing contacts a reminder:

- 7 days and 3 days before the due date
- The day after the due date, when the grace period starts
- 3 days before the grace period ends

If the invoice still isn't paid when the grace period ends, the subscription expires, the invoice is voided and your district is deleted (see below). The subscription won't expire while an ACH or bank transfer payment is still settling. To use Tidal PTC again after an expiry, place a new order in the billing portal.

## When a subscription expires

<Aside type="danger">
When a subscription expires, including when an unpaid invoice's grace period ends, the district and all of its data (schools, users, time slots, bookings and activity) are permanently deleted, usually within minutes. This can't be undone. Renew before the expiry date, and export anything you need to keep beforehand.
</Aside>
