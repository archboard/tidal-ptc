ClassLink is planned as a second way for Tidal PTC to get its people and classes, alongside [PowerSchool](/integrations/powerschool/). It is not available yet, so it doesn't appear as an option for **SIS data provider** in [Tenant settings](/district-admin/tenant-settings/).

<Aside type="tip" title="Does your district use ClassLink?">
We'd like to hear from schools and districts that integrate with ClassLink. Knowing who needs it, and how you use it, helps us build the right support. Email us at [support@archboard.io](mailto:support@archboard.io?subject=ClassLink%20support) to get in touch.
</Aside>

## Until then

PowerSchool SIS is the only supported provider. Districts that use ClassLink can't connect Tidal PTC to it yet.

## Following progress

To follow ClassLink support, watch the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/archboard/tidal-ptc/issues) or comment on an existing ClassLink issue.
