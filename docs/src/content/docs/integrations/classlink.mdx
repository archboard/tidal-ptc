Tidal PTC lets staff and guardians sign in with ClassLink, alongside password and [PowerSchool](/integrations/powerschool/) sign-in. ClassLink is available for **sign-in** only. It is not yet a **SIS data provider**: your schools, people and classes still come from PowerSchool.

## Who this is for

District admins and IT staff who want people to sign in with ClassLink.

## How it works

Tidal PTC signs people in through Archboard's sign-in service. Your district doesn't create a ClassLink application or enter any client IDs or secrets. People sign in with ClassLink, the service confirms who they are, and Tidal PTC signs them in.

## Before you start

- A ClassLink staff account for your district, to connect it.
- The **edit tenant settings** permission in Tidal PTC.
- Self-hosted installs only: see [Self-hosted installs](#self-hosted-installs) first.

## Connect ClassLink

<Steps>
1. Open **Settings**, then **Tenant settings**, and find the **ClassLink** card.

   <Screenshot name="settings-tenant" alt="Tenant settings page with the Tenant Settings, SMTP Settings, PowerSchool plugin, ClassLink and Active schools cards" />

2. Select **Connect ClassLink** and sign in with ClassLink as a staff account.
3. Tidal PTC saves your district's ClassLink tenant ID. The card shows "Connected ClassLink tenant: " followed by the ID.
</Steps>

To switch to a different ClassLink district, select **Reconnect ClassLink**. It replaces the saved tenant ID.

## Sign in with ClassLink

Once connected, the login page shows **Sign in with ClassLink**.

<Screenshot name="login" alt="The Tidal PTC sign-in page with Sign in with ClassLink and Sign in with PowerSchool buttons" />

- **Staff and guardians** can sign in. **Students cannot.**
- The person must **already exist in Tidal PTC**. ClassLink sign-in doesn't create accounts.
- Tidal PTC matches the ClassLink email address to the Tidal PTC user's email, ignoring case. ClassLink staff, teacher and admin roles match a staff account. ClassLink parent and guardian roles match a guardian account.
- Only people from your connected ClassLink district are accepted.

Sign-in with ClassLink works alongside password and PowerSchool sign-in, and doesn't depend on which **SIS data provider** you use.

## Cloud and dedicated

Nothing else to do. Archboard registers your domains, including custom domains, automatically.

## Self-hosted installs

A self-hosted install registers itself with Archboard's sign-in service. For that:

- The install needs its **license key**.
- It must be reachable from the internet over HTTPS on its public domain.
- `https://<your domain>/.well-known/auth-broker-challenge/tidal-ptc` must be reachable and must not redirect. Registration proves you control the domain by fetching that address, so make sure no proxy or firewall blocks it.

Registration happens in one of two ways:

- On the install page, tick **Enable ClassLink sign-in** and enter your license key. See [First run](/installation/first-run/).
- Each time an admin selects **Connect ClassLink**, Tidal PTC registers again using the license key saved on the tenant.

If you change your domain, select **Connect ClassLink** again to register the new one.

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "ClassLink sign-in failed." | The person isn't a Tidal PTC user with a matching email and user type, is a student, or belongs to a different ClassLink district. Check the user's email in Tidal PTC. |
| "Add your license key before connecting ClassLink." | Self-hosted only: the tenant has no license key saved. |
| "ClassLink registration failed: ..." | Self-hosted only: the reason follows the colon. Check the HTTPS, domain and `.well-known` requirements above, then select **Connect ClassLink** again. |
