Tidal PTC (parent-teacher conferences) is a web application for scheduling parent-teacher conferences. Admins and teachers create time slots, and parents and guardians sign in to book them. It is built by Archboard, licensed under AGPL-3.0, and the source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/archboard/tidal-ptc). This page explains who uses it, what it does, and where to go next.

<Screenshot name="dashboard-guardian" alt="A guardian's dashboard listing each student's teachers with a Book link next to every teacher who has open time slots" />

## Who uses it

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| District admin | Connects PowerSchool, chooses the active schools, configures email, and manages users and permissions across the district. |
| School staff | Create and manage time slots for their school, book conferences on behalf of families, handle translator requests, and review activity. |
| Teacher | Signs in with PowerSchool, sees their upcoming conferences, and (when allowed) creates their own time slots. |
| Guardian | Signs in, picks a student, and books, reschedules or cancels a conference with each of the student's teachers. |

## Features

- **PowerSchool integration.** Install the plugin and schools, sections, students, teachers and contacts sync automatically, with single sign-on for everyone. No CSV exports or temporary passwords.
- **Flexible time slots.** Create single slots or batches, and control when booking opens and closes for families.
- **Translators.** Families can request a translator, and staff assign translators to conferences.
- **Online meetings.** Conferences can be held online as well as in person.
- **Email reminders** for upcoming conferences.
- **Multilingual.** The interface is available in English, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Spanish and Arabic.
- **Activity log.** A record of bookings and changes to time slots.
- **Free to self-host**, or hosted for you by Archboard.
