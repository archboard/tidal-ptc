_Effective November 20, 2026_

This policy explains how Archboard handles information when it hosts Tidal PTC for a school district through Cloud or Private dedicated hosting, and when you use this documentation site.

<Aside type="note" title="The short version">
Archboard only provides hosting. Your district owns its data and controls the application. Archboard does not access, view, use, analyze, share or sell the data stored in your Tidal PTC site.
</Aside>

## Our role

Your district (or school) is the owner and controller of the data in its Tidal PTC site. It decides who has an account, what is synced from its student information system, and how the application is used. Archboard runs the servers that the application runs on, and nothing more.

For schools in the United States, Archboard acts as a service provider under the district's direct control, consistent with the "school official" exception of the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). Archboard uses student records only to provide hosting, and does not re-disclose them.

## Data stored in Tidal PTC

Depending on how your district sets it up, a Tidal PTC site may store:

- Names, email addresses, preferred language and time zone of staff and parents or guardians
- Student names, numbers, grades and the relationships between students and their contacts, usually synced from PowerSchool or ClassLink
- Schools, time slots, bookings, notes, meeting links and translator requests
- Activity logs of changes made in the application
- Settings the district enters, such as SMTP credentials, which are stored encrypted

This data is entered or synced by your district and its users. It is stored on the servers that host your site.

## We do not access your data

Archboard staff do not sign in to your site, browse its database, or read, export or analyze the data in it. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, training artificial intelligence models, or any purpose other than keeping the service running.

Running the service involves automated systems that handle your data without anyone looking at it, such as encrypted backups, server monitoring and security updates. The only exception is if we are legally compelled to disclose data, for example by a court order. If that happens, we will notify your district first unless the law forbids it.

If your district asks for help, it decides what information to share with us, and we use it only to resolve that request.

## Data we collect directly

Archboard keeps a small amount of information about its customers so it can sell and support hosting:

- **Billing records:** the district's name, billing contacts, purchase orders, invoices and subscription dates, collected through the [billing portal](https://billing.archboard.io/get-started). Payments are handled by a third-party payment processor; Archboard does not store full card or bank numbers.
- **Support messages:** anything you send us when you contact us.
- **This documentation site:** standard server logs, such as IP address, browser and pages requested, used to keep the site working and secure. The site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Security

Each district's data is kept separate. On Cloud hosting, districts share servers but not data. On Private dedicated hosting, each district has its own servers and database. Connections are encrypted in transit, sensitive settings are encrypted at rest, and servers receive regular security updates.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your district's data, we will notify your district promptly so it can meet any obligations it has to families and staff.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your site for as long as your district's subscription is active. When a subscription ends, the district and all of its data are permanently deleted, as described in [Buying and renewing](/cloud/billing/#when-a-subscription-expires). Backups containing the data age out on their normal schedule after that.

Billing records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.

## Self-hosted installs

If your district hosts Tidal PTC itself, Archboard has no access to your servers or data and receives nothing from your installation. Your district's own policies apply.

## Requests from parents, guardians, students and staff

Because your district controls the data, questions or requests to see, correct or delete information should go to your school or district. If you contact Archboard directly, we will refer you to them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we change the effective date at the top of this page, and we notify customers of significant changes by email.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Archboard through [archboard.io](https://archboard.io).
