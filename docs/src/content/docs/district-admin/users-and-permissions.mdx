The **Users** page lists everyone at your current school who is synced from your SIS: staff, contacts (guardians) and students. From there you can view a user's calendar, turn booking on or off for them, and edit their permissions. Permissions decide what each person can see and change; most are granted per school, a few apply to the whole district.

## Who this is for

District administrators, and school staff with the **Edit user permissions** permission.

## Before you start

- To see the **Users** menu item you need **View list** on **Users** for the school.
- To change permissions you need **Edit user permissions**.
- Permissions you grant apply to your current school. To grant permissions at another school, switch to it first (see [Concepts](/getting-started/concepts/)).

## How permissions work

Tidal PTC uses roles and permissions:

- The first user created during [installation](/installation/first-run/) gets the **district admin** role, which has every permission in every active school.
- Everyone else starts with no admin permissions. Users come from your SIS; you grant permissions to them individually on the permissions page.
- Teachers who have sections assigned in the SIS can own time slots without any extra permission.

Most permissions are **scoped to a school**: granting one at School A gives nothing at School B. Two permissions are district-wide: **Manages tenancy** and **Edit tenant settings**. See the [permissions reference](/reference/permissions/) for the full list.

## Find a user

<Steps>
1. Select **Users** in the navigation.
   <Screenshot name="users" alt="Users list with name, email and type columns and a filter bar" />
2. Search by name or email, or open the filters to filter by **First name**, **Last name** or **User type**, then select **Apply filters**.
3. Select a name to open the user's page. It shows their time slots on a calendar in your timezone.
   <Screenshot name="user-show" alt="A staff member's page showing their time slots on a calendar" />
</Steps>

If the user can own time slots, their page has a **Manage time slots** button. See [Time slots and batches](/staff/time-slots/).

## Turn booking on or off

A user with booking disabled shows a **Booking disabled** label, and their sections show the label next to the teacher. When you create time slots for a batch of teachers, users with booking disabled are skipped. Time slots they already have are not affected. To stop families booking for particular classes, disable booking on the section or course instead (see [Directory](/staff/directory/)).

- For one user, open the row's menu and select **Disable booking** or **Enable booking**.
- For several, check their rows, open **Actions** above the table and select **Disable booking** or **Enable booking**.

You need **Update** on **Users** to change booking status.

## Edit a user's permissions

<Steps>
1. On the **Users** page, open the user's row menu and select **Edit permissions**.
2. Check or uncheck permissions. Each change saves as soon as you click it; there is no save button.
</Steps>

The page has two parts.

### General Permissions

This part only appears if you have **Manages tenancy** yourself. It holds the district-wide permissions:

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Manages tenancy** | Full access to the entire tenancy with full permissions for everything. |
| **Edit tenant settings** | Edit all [tenant settings](/district-admin/tenant-settings/). |

### School Permissions

These apply to your current school only.

- **Grant all permissions for this school** gives the user full access at this school.
- **Edit school settings**: edit all [school settings](/district-admin/school-settings/).
- **Edit user permissions**: open this page and change other users' permissions.
- **Own time slots**: the user can have reservable time slots even without assigned sections.

Below these, each type of record has its own block: **Users**, **Courses**, **Sections**, **Students** and **Time Slots**. Check **Full access** for everything on that record type, or pick individual permissions:

| Permission | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **View list** | See the listing page. |
| **View** | See an individual record. |
| **Create** | Create records. Time slots only. |
| **Update** | Change records. |
| **Delete** | Delete records. Time slots only. |

Users, courses, sections and students come from the SIS, so they only offer **View list**, **View** and **Update**.

<Aside type="tip">
For office staff who book conferences on behalf of families, grant **View list**, **View** and **Update** on **Time Slots**, plus **View list** and **View** on **Students**. See [Booking for families](/staff/reservations/).
</Aside>
